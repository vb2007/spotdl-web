# v40.1 — Breaker Wedge Fix (hard failures end by themselves)

Branch: `dev-breaker-wedge-fix` → PR into `main`
Version: `4.40.1`

## Scope

Inserted by the owner on 2026-10-13, after v40 merged and before v41. It fixes what v39.2 (PR #57,
`4.39.2`) left unfinished. v39.2 classified age-gated and no-match tracks as terminal. That
classification is right, but on production it was mostly unreachable.

**The defect, from production's own rows (read-only, 2026-10-13, running `4.40.0`).** The Scooter
artist job `6f1640ce` had **16 tracks `waiting`**, and the global breaker was wedged:
`worker_state` = `consecutive_failures 10`, `breaker_trip_count 6`, paused for 6h. Every user's new
jobs waited behind it. The track the owner looked at (`0404e6a0`, "Fuck The Millennium",
*24 Carat Gold*) **had** reached `lookup_failed` ("Age-restricted on YouTube", IPv6 rung, on its first
attempt after v39.2 deployed). Four more copies of that song, on other albums, and 12 more tracks
were still waiting. Four causes:

1. **The breaker never re-armed.** `consecutive_failures` only reset on a success, and a manual
   release kept it on purpose. After every release, **one** breaker-feeding failure re-tripped at
   the top step (6h). A queue of tracks that can never succeed never produces that success, so each
   6h window got about one real attempt. The rest were `held` until the next release, forever.
2. **Dead proxies fed the breaker.** 9 of the 16 tracks had `attempt_count ≥ 2`, the proxy rung.
   All 10 enabled proxies had `last_success_at` NULL. The re-trip at 18:01 came from
   `ProxyError … Unable to connect to proxy (ConnectTimeout)`. That error is raised while
   ytmusicapi builds its client, before any search, so v39.2 wrapped it as a breaker-feeding
   `no_output`. With proxy cooldown capped at 4h and the track ladder at 24h, a proxy-rung track
   met another dead proxy on every attempt and **never reached a verdict**.
3. **A terminal verdict through a proxy was charged to the proxy.** C.I.F.L.'s search through proxy
   `fa02ff90` completed ("No results"). It was the first proxy on record to carry a request, and it
   still got a failure and a cooldown.
4. Tracks that failed before v39.2 carry bare "no output file" messages, and only a fresh attempt
   reclassifies them. Causes 1 and 2 starved exactly those attempts.

**Owner decisions (2026-10-13).**
- **Fresh breaker streak per trip, and on manual release.** Escalation (`breaker_trip_count`) still
  only resets on a success. This edits CLAUDE.md's "Retry engine numbers" (owner-approved).
- **Hard failures stay terminal and get no retry attention**: age gate, no match, unavailable
  video. "No matter how many times or what the app will try with, restriction won't magically
  disappear nor the track'll come online." Fetching them some other way (fallback providers, a
  cookie file) is **not** in scope.

## Tasks

1. **Fresh streak per trip.** `retry.maybe_trip_breaker` zeroes `consecutive_failures` when it trips.
   The alert keeps the count it tripped on. `POST /api/worker/breaker/release` zeroes it too and
   keeps `breaker_trip_count`.
2. **Proxy transport failures are the proxy's, not the track's or the breaker's.** Add
   `retry.ProxyTransportError` and `retry.is_proxy_transport_error(exc, via_proxy=…)`.
   - Proxy-specific errors always count: requests', urllib3's and yt-dlp's `ProxyError`, and
     yt-dlp's "Unable to connect to proxy", "Tunnel connection failed" and "407 Proxy
     Authentication".
   - On a proxied attempt, any connection-level failure counts too: aborted, reset, refused or
     timed out, requests'/urllib3's/yt-dlp's transport errors.
   - In that case `download_track` records an attempt `failed`/`other`/`proxy`, cools the proxy
     down, and calls `retry.record_transport_failure`: the track is `waiting` until the ladder's
     first step, `attempt_count` is **unchanged**, and the breaker hears nothing.
   - That rung-free retry is granted **once in a row**. A read timeout or reset can also happen
     after YouTube was reached, so a second consecutive transport failure goes through
     `record_failure`: it consumes the rung, is still `other`, and still doesn't feed the breaker.
     The messages carry `attempts.TRANSPORT_FAILURE_PREFIX`, which the failure-spike alert leaves
     out.
   - `download_one` chains the error it replaces, so the task can see the original. The chain
     walk follows yt-dlp's `DownloadError.exc_info`.
   - Direct rungs keep their old classification.
3. **Probe before use.** `proxies.pick_proxy` TCP-probes each LRU candidate (`_probe_reachable`,
   2s), cools down the ones that don't accept, and returns None when none do. `download_track`'s
   existing direct-IPv4 fallback then applies. `download_track` commits before the probes rather
   than holding a transaction open across them, and re-checks for a cancel afterwards.
4. **A terminal verdict through a proxy credits the proxy** (`record_proxy_result(success=True)`).
5. **Hard-failure marker sweep** in `downloads._swallowed_error_to_raise`, matched
   case-insensitively.
   - Age gate: "confirm your age", "age-restricted", "inappropriate for some users".
   - New `UnavailableVideoError(LookupError)`: private, removed, terminated account,
     members-only.
   - Rate-limit and bot-check wording stays on the ladder and keeps feeding the breaker: "not a
     bot", "try again later", "This content isn't available", a bare "Video unavailable", 429, 403.
     So does **geo-blocking**: it depends on the egress IP, so another rung can change it.
     Each marker and each exclusion gets its own test.
6. **Docs.**
   - CLAUDE.md "Retry engine numbers" (breaker and proxy bullets).
   - `docs/GOTCHAS.md`: v40.1 entries, plus a dated correction on the v39.2 line.
   - The master plan's Amendments: v40.1, and v39.2 retroactively.

## Out of scope

- Fetching age-gated or not-on-YouTube-Music tracks some other way (fallback audio providers,
  `COOKIE_FILE`). This would be a separately planned slice if the owner wants it.
- Disabling production's never-successful proxies. That is the owner's call in the UI, and the
  probe already makes them harmless.
- `tracks.scheduled_at` left stale on a `lookup_failed` track: the rollup ignores it, so it is
  cosmetic. Logged under "Found, not fixed".

## Deploy notes

- No migration, no new env var.
- **After the deploy, press "release now"** (admin). It now starts a fresh streak. Without it, the
  stale `consecutive_failures=10` costs one more 6h trip at the next scheduled release before the
  reset takes effect.

## Done when

Each bullet needs its own evidence from the real local stack (`spotdlwebtest`, real network, login
through the real upstream), except the last one, which is post-deploy.

- [ ] One real breaker-feeding failure at streak 4 trips the breaker and leaves the streak at 0.
      A failure after the release leaves the streak at 1 with no re-trip.
- [ ] An admin release zeroes the streak and keeps `breaker_trip_count`. A non-admin gets 403.
- [ ] A proxy that doesn't accept a TCP connection is cooled down at pick time, and the attempt goes
      out `direct-ipv4`. The breaker is unchanged.
- [ ] A proxy that accepts and then drops the connection gives an attempt `proxy`/`other`:
      `attempt_count` unchanged, breaker unchanged, proxy cooled. A second one in a row consumes
      the rung. The next dispatch reaches a verdict.
- [ ] Through a working proxy, an age-gated track and a no-match track both end `lookup_failed`. The
      proxy gets `last_success_at`, no cooldown, and the breaker is unchanged.
- [ ] Production's real stuck tracks (age-gated, no-match, the 63-min DJ mix) end terminal on both
      direct rungs with no breaker increment. A control track completes.
- [ ] Full `pytest` is green, including the cross-user sweep files.
- [ ] Post-deploy (read-only prod DB): after "release now", the 16 stuck tracks reach a terminal
      state, job `6f1640ce` reads `settled · partial`, and the breaker doesn't re-trip.
